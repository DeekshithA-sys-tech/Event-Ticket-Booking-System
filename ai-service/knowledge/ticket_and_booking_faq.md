# EventPulse Ticketing and Booking FAQ

## Booking Process & Seat Reservations
- **How does seat selection work?** 
  EventPulse provides an interactive visual seat layout. When you click an available seat, a temporary 10-minute hold lock is placed on that seat in our real-time database to prevent other users from booking it while you complete checkout.
- **What happens if my 10-minute timer expires?** 
  If payment is not completed within 10 minutes, the seat hold automatically expires and releases back into the pool for other customers.
- **What is the maximum number of tickets per transaction?** 
  To prevent bulk buying and black marketing, customers can purchase a maximum of **6 tickets per transaction** for any single event.

## Digital Passes & PDF Delivery
- **Where can I find my ticket after payment?** 
  Immediately after successful booking confirmation, your digital pass with a cryptographic QR code is generated. You can view and download it directly under the "My Bookings" section of your EventPulse account, and a copy is emailed to your registered address.
- **Do I need to print my ticket?** 
  No. Mobile digital QR codes displayed on your smartphone screen can be scanned directly at the entry gate. However, printed PDF copies are also accepted.

## Group Bookings & Corporate Inquiries
- For bookings of more than 20 attendees or corporate sponsorships, contact the EventPulse enterprise team at `corporate@eventpulse.io` for custom invoicing, dedicated hospitality boxes, and volume discounts.

## Lost Tickets or Technical Issues
- If you lost access to your confirmation email or your QR code will not load, simply log in to your EventPulse web portal or visit the venue box office / help desk with your photo ID and booking reference number (e.g., `EP-12345`) to receive a reprint.
