# EventPulse Cancellation and Refund Policy

## Overview
At EventPulse, we aim to provide a fair and transparent refund and cancellation experience for all event attendees and organizers. This document outlines the official eligibility rules, timelines, and deductions for ticket cancellations and event disruptions.

## Cancellation Timelines & Refund Percentages
Ticket refund eligibility depends on how many hours or days prior to the event start time the cancellation request is initiated:

- **More than 7 days before event start:** 
  Attendees receive a **100% full refund** to the original payment method, minus nominal third-party payment processing gateway fees (2.5%).
- **Between 48 hours and 7 days before event start:** 
  Attendees receive an **80% partial refund**. A 20% cancellation and administrative fee applies.
- **Between 24 hours and 48 hours before event start:** 
  Attendees receive a **50% partial refund**.
- **Less than 24 hours before event start:** 
  **Non-refundable**. No cancellations or refunds can be issued within 24 hours of the scheduled start time as venue organizers finalize headcount and security logistics.

## Event Postponements & Cancellations by Organizers
- **Event Cancelled by Organizer:** If an event is cancelled by the organizer or artist, all ticket holders are automatically issued a **100% full refund** including all platform service fees within 5 to 7 business days.
- **Event Rescheduled:** If an event date or venue is rescheduled, existing tickets automatically remain valid for the new date. Attendees who cannot make the rescheduled date are entitled to request a 100% refund within 14 days of the rescheduling announcement.

## Refund Processing Times
- Refunds are credited directly back to the original payment source (credit card, debit card, UPI, or net banking).
- Standard processing time is **5 to 7 working business days**.
- Cash refunds are strictly not supported.

## Ticket Resale and Name Transfers
- Tickets can be transferred to another user via their registered EventPulse email address up to **12 hours before** event doors open.
- Unauthorized scalping or reselling above face value is strictly prohibited and results in immediate ticket voidance without refund.
