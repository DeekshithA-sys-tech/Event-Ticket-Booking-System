# EventPulse Venue Guidelines, Entry Rules & Accessibility

## General Entry Requirements
- **Valid ID Proof:** All attendees must present a valid government-issued photo ID (Passport, Driver's License, National ID, or Student ID for student discounts) matching the primary attendee name on the ticket.
- **Digital E-Ticket:** Attendees must show their official EventPulse digital QR pass on their mobile device or printed PDF pass at the turnstiles. Screen captures or blurry photos may be rejected by barcode scanners.
- **Entry Age Restrictions:** 
  - Standard concerts and theater performances are open to all ages unless marked 18+.
  - Events labeled **18+ or 21+** require physical age verification ID at entry. Minors will not be admitted and tickets are non-refundable in such cases.

## Bag Policy & Security Checks
- **Permitted Bags:** Clear vinyl or plastic tote bags (max dimensions: 12" x 12" x 6") and small clutch purses (max dimensions: 4.5" x 6.5") are permitted.
- **Prohibited Items:**
  - Professional cameras with detachable telephoto lenses (unless with official press accreditation).
  - Outside food, glass bottles, metal cans, and alcoholic beverages.
  - Laser pointers, whistles, noisemakers, fireworks, or hazardous items.
  - Large backpacks, luggage, or umbrellas with pointed metal tips.
- **Security Check:** All attendees are subject to bag searches and metal detector screening prior to entering the venue.

## Parking & Public Transit
- **On-site Parking:** Most premium venues offer dedicated visitor parking. Parking passes can be pre-booked online through the EventPulse event page or purchased at the gate subject to availability.
- **Designated Ride-share Zones:** Venues feature marked pickup and drop-off points for Uber, Lyft, and local taxis near Gate 2.

## Accessibility (ADA & Special Needs)
- **Wheelchair Accessibility:** All partner venues provide ADA-compliant ramp access, dedicated wheelchair viewing bays in Tier A/VIP sections, and accessible restrooms.
- **Assisted Listening Devices:** Available at the venue guest relations desk upon request free of charge with a valid ID deposit.
- **Companion Seating:** Each wheelchair ticket includes the option to reserve one adjacent companion seat at the same tier price.
